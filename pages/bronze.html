Your Bronze Award

Bronze is the first level of the Award, and you can start it in the school year that you turn 14. Bronze takes a minimum of 6 months to complete.
There are three specific sections—Voluntary Service, Skills and Physical Recreation—that you'll choose your activities from, plus an Adventurous Journey to complete.
You’ll have the chance to help others or the environment, get fitter, learn valuable skills and explore the countryside.
Whatever you choose to do for your DofE, you’ll make friends for life, have amazing experiences and enhance your CV and college or university application.
Here you’ll find out how the Award levels work, why they are important and what you can do to make the most of this fantastic opportunity.
Anyone aged 14 to 24 can take part in three progressive levels of programmes leading to a Bronze, Silver or Gold DofE Award.
To achieve their Bronze DofE Award, your child will need to complete their own programme of activities in these four sections:
- Volunteering
Supporting individuals or
giving back to the community.
- Physical
Improving in an area of
sport, dance or fitness.
- Skills
Developing practical and social
skills and personal interests.
- Expedition
Planning, training for and
completing an adventurous journey.

How long does it take?

You can expect each activity to take one hour a week over a set period of time. It’s easy for your child to fit them in around their studies and social life.
They’ll need to participate regularly and be committed to their DofE during this time.

What will my child gain?

There’s not enough space here to list all the benefits so here’s a quick snapshot:
– Open doors to further education or employment.
– Help to make their community a better place to live.
– Get healthier and happier.
– Become more confident and motivated to succeed at school and beyond.
– Learn how to overcome challenges and be more self-sufficient and resilient.
– Develop positive relationships with teachers outside of the classroom.
– Improve their social skills and make new friends.
